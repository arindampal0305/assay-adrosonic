Terms of Service & Data Usage

How ASSAY protects your commercial insurance data

1. Enterprise Data Isolation & Privacy

All uploaded Statements of Values (SOV) spreadsheets are processed in temporary, isolated RAM sandboxes. No customer data is retained or used to train third-party LLMs.

2. Zero Hallucination Guarantee

Data values and column schema transformations are executed using deterministic code. Missing values are flagged rather than artificially invented.

3. Human Underwriter Control

Underwriters maintain 100% decision authority over all proposed column header mappings and data quality recommendations before exporting.
